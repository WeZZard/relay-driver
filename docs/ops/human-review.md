# Review a delivered trajectory

1. Open the accepted local package and inspect its recording completeness and execution outcome separately.
2. Select the step relevant to the behavior being judged.
3. Watch the input and its visible result with the synchronized explanation.
4. Inspect the verified frame or uncertainty interval when the exact observation matters.
5. Follow supporting logs and file assertions with their evidence sources identified.
6. Inspect failed attempts and continuations without treating a later retry as a replacement for the original.
7. Record the human judgment against the specific evidence reviewed.

An agent's successful execution verdict does not supply human approval. Direct accessibility activation establishes only the accessibility behavior exercised; pointer and keyboard claims require their corresponding input evidence.

The [review flow](../ux/review-flow.md) describes the viewer interaction. The [verification catalog](../engineering/verification.md) defines the associated review contracts without claiming they have run.
